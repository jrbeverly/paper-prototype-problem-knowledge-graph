title: AI-Generated Initial Graph
relationships:
  - constrains: knowledge-object
  - references: problem-knowledge-graph
---

The first version of any graph should be produced automatically by an AI that decomposes existing documentation into draft knowledge objects. Humans then review, refine, and extend that draft rather than authoring the graph from scratch. This minimises the cost of adopting the approach on an existing project.
